# Desk research: does the problem exist in Leeuwarden?

> **Collected:** 2026-10-05 (Mon) · **Purpose:** validate the Which Bin? problem (everyday sorting + bulky waste) before building more.
> **Rule:** every claim below has a source. Nothing here is a sorting rule for the game until it's checked again on the day it goes in.

---

## 1. Key source: the municipality's own resident survey

**"Goed Terecht in Leeuwarden – Bewonersenquête"** (Ipsos I&O for Gemeente Leeuwarden)
[Source PDF](https://leeuwarden.bestuurlijkeinformatie.nl/Document/View/f7e4a38a-b44b-44bd-9a4d-d0bf4388b133)

- **Method:** 4,000 randomly selected residents were invited by letter. Fieldwork ran from late March to 30 April 2024. **n = 1,204** (30% response), weighted on age, sex and neighbourhood (pp. 5–6).
- **Limit:** the report has **no breakdown by nationality, language or student status.** It splits by age (18–29), household type and bin type. Internationals aren't visible in this data, which is the gap our own research fills.

### Everyday sorting

| Finding | Number | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Residents who have a waste type they **don't know what to do with** | **21%** | p. 13 |
| Most-named confusing items | Plastic (e.g. bottles without a deposit), broken clothing, **multi-material packaging "zoals pizzadozen"**, unwanted or broken electrical appliances | p. 13 |
| Food scraps put in residual waste instead of GFT | 23% (peel: 18%) | p. 10 |
| Share of residual waste that is food scraps (2023 sorting analysis) | 20% | p. 10 |
| 18–29-year-olds | **Sort GFT, clothing and small electricals less often** | p. 10 |
| Main reasons for not sorting (all residents) | Too little to bother (40%), **too much hassle (29%)**, no space at home (25%), dirty to store (22%) … **"I don't know the rules" (7%)** | p. 11 |
| 18–29-year-olds | **More often say sorting is "too much hassle"** | p. 11 |
| Agree that sorting is useful | 94% | p. 12 |

**What this means for us:**
- Pizza boxes being confusing is confirmed by the municipality's own data. Keep it as the example item.
- Among residents in general, not knowing the rules is a **small** reason (7%). Hassle and lack of space are bigger. A pure knowledge game addresses a minority of the problem for the general population. **Open question:** is the knowledge share much higher for internationals? This survey can't answer that. Our card sort and interviews can.

### Bags next to containers and dumped bulky waste

| Finding | Number | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Put a bag or **something else** next to a container in the past year | 6% of all residents | p. 24 |
| … among **18–29-year-olds** | **14%** (significantly higher) | p. 25 |
| … among people using a shared underground container (*verzamelcontainer*) | 16% (vs 2% with a home bin) | p. 25 |
| Main reason | Container full or stuck (71%) | p. 26 |
| Residents who **regularly** saw dumped bulky waste in 2023 | **about 1 in 3** (32%) | p. 27 |
| Share of Binnenstad (city centre) residents using a shared container | 86% | p. 45 |
| Share of 18–29-year-olds using a shared container | 40% (vs 21–27% of older groups) | p. 45 |

### Bulky waste (*grofvuil*)

| Finding | Number | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Residents who **don't know the bulky-waste rules well** | **1 in 5** | p. 8 |
| Knew about the 2 free pickups a year | 82% overall · **75% of 18–29s** | p. 35 |
| Knew sorted bulky waste can always go to the milieustraat for free | 67% overall · **58% of under-55s** vs 80% of 55+ | p. 35 |
| Dissatisfied with the bulky-waste options | 31% | p. 36 |

**What this means for us:** young people know the bulky-waste options less well and leave things next to containers more often. Dumped bulky waste is visible to a third of residents. That supports adding a bulky-waste scenario. It's a real local problem, not one we invented.

## 2. How bulky waste works in Leeuwarden (to verify before it goes into the game)

| What | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Pickup | Book through Omrin (portal, Afval app, or 0900-2100215). **Free twice a year on special collection days, max 1 m³.** Another date or a third pickup costs **€63 per m³** | [Gemeente Leeuwarden (EN)](https://www.leeuwarden.nl/en/waste-and-recycling/grofvuil-ophalen-of-wegbrengen/) |
| Milieustraat | Greunsweg 86. Mon–Fri 09:00–16:30, Sat 09:00–17:30. **Appointment required. Bring your *milieupas*.** | [Omrin: Milieustraat Leeuwarden](https://www.omrin.nl/zelf-regelen/milieustraten/afval-voor-de-milieustraat/leeuwarden) |
| Milieustraat cost | Free if separated by material. Unsorted is free twice a year, then €6.15–€41 depending on load | Gemeente Leeuwarden page above |
| No car? | The milieustraat **lends trailers (€100 deposit) and cargo bikes (€50 deposit) for free** | Omrin page above |
| Bag next to a container | Counts as illegal dumping and can be fined. Amounts differ per municipality; one source gives about €100 on average. **Leeuwarden's actual amount not yet found.** | [MAX Meldpunt](https://www.maxmeldpunt.nl/wonen/afval-te-vroeg-of-te-laat-buiten-zetten-of-naast-de-volle-container-dit-kunt-u-verwachten/) |

**Friction points an international is likely to hit** (hypotheses to test in interviews):
1. You need an appointment **and** a *milieupas*. Do students in shared or rented housing have one, or does the landlord?
2. Pickup is limited to **special collection days**, which may not line up with a move-out date. Outside them it costs money.
3. No car: the free cargo bike or trailer exists, but would anyone know?
4. The booking flow is through Omrin. Is it in English? *(Not checked yet.)*

## 3. What internationals say online

- Expat guides say the confusion comes from rules **differing per municipality** and quote Reddit threads full of it ([NLCompass](https://www.nlcompass.com/guides/waste-recycling-trash-netherlands), [Strooptaal](https://strooptaal.app/blog/dutch-garbage-disposal-rules-guide-to-afvalscheiding-2026)). These are secondary sources, so don't quote them as evidence of the problem.
- **No first-hand posts found for Leeuwarden.** Reddit and Facebook groups aren't indexed well or need a login. **That's your job:** see `02-validation-kit.md` §1.

## 4. Verdict so far

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| Sorting rules confuse residents, including on pizza boxes and electricals | ✅ Supported (municipal survey, 21%) |
| Young residents sort less and dump next to containers more | ✅ Supported (18–29) |
| Bulky waste is a visible local problem, and young people know the options less | ✅ Supported |
| **Internationals specifically** struggle more | ❓ **Unknown.** Not in any data found. Must come from our card sort and interviews. |
| The cause is a **knowledge gap** (not hassle) | ⚠️ **Weak for residents in general** (7%). Needs testing for internationals. |
