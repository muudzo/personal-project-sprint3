## 1. The real bin set (replaces the placeholder `GFT, Paper, PMD, Residual, Glass`)

| Bin | Who has it | Takes | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sortibak** (grey bin) or **restafvalcontainer** | Houses: Sortibak at home. Apartments + city centre: shared container, opened with the **milieupas** | Residual waste **plus plastic, cans (blik) and drink cartons (drinkpakken)**. Omrin sorts these out afterwards by machine | S1 pp. 4–5, 8–9, 16–17 · S2 |
| **Biobak** or **gft-zuil** | Houses / shared container (milieupas) | Vegetable, fruit and garden waste + food leftovers ("gft en etensresten") | S1 pp. 6–9 · S2 |
| **Papierbak** or paper container | Many houses ("vaak ook") / shared container | Paper and cardboard, "altijd schoon en droog" | S1 pp. 4–5, 10–11 · S2 |
| **Glasbak** (glass container) | Shared containers | Glass bottles and jars; "mag vies en met deksel" | S1 pp. 10–11 · S2 |
| **Textielbak** (textile container) | Shared containers | Clothes and textiles, also worn or broken | S1 pp. 12–13 · S2 |
| *Not a bin:* **kca** (red kca-box → chemokar, or milieustraat) | Every household has a red kca-box | Small chemical waste: batteries, paint. The chemokar also takes small electrical items | S1 pp. 4–7 · S2 |
| *Not a bin:* **milieustraat** (recycling centre) | Book a time slot, bring your milieupas | Bulky waste, electrical appliances, mattresses, frying fat, hard plastics, polystyrene… | S1 pp. 12–15 |

**Colours:** only the Sortibak's colour is stated ("Sortibak (grijze bak)", S1 pp. 4–5; "grijze bak", S2). The Biobak and Papierbak colours are **not stated** in these sources. Check on the street or ask Omrin before the game uses colour.

### The rule most likely to surprise internationals
In Leeuwarden you **don't** separate plastic. "Plastic, blik en drinkpakken horen in je grijze bak (Sortibak) of in de restafvalcontainer" (S2). "Met high tech machines halen we de vele verschillende soorten én maten er namelijk achteraf uit" (S1 pp. 16–17). Many other Dutch cities, and many home countries, ask you to separate plastic, so this one is invisible until someone tells you.

## 3. Other rules worth a "why" line in the game

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| A bin with the wrong waste in it is **not emptied**; loose bags next to the bin aren't collected | S1 pp. 4–5 |
| Waste left **next to a shared container counts as illegal dumping**: risk of a fine | S1 pp. 6–7 |
| You **pay per emptying**: Sortibak €1 per emptying + €0.20 per kilo; shared container €1.15 per bag (2026). Sorting well literally saves money | S1 pp. 4–5 |
| Put the bin out **before 07:30** (city centre: 08:00) and take it back the same day | S1 pp. 4–5 |
| No plastic bags in the Biobak, **not even biodegradable ones** | S2 |
| Glass may be **dirty and still have its lid on** | S1 pp. 10–11 · S2 |
| Paper must be **clean and dry** | S2 |
| Bulky-waste pickup: **free up to 2 times a year** on fixed route days, after booking; otherwise €63; max 1 m³. Unbooked bulky waste counts as illegal dumping | S1 pp. 16–17 |
| Milieustraten: **Leeuwarden** (Greunsweg 86), **Grou** (Oedsmawei 9), **Stiens** (Seerob 23). Book a time slot and bring your milieupas. Free trailer or cargo bike to borrow | S1 pp. 14–15 |
| Reusable items: **Omrin Estafette Recycle Boulevard**, Voltastraat 11 | S1 pp. 2–3 |
