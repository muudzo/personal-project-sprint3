> **Goal:** find out whether **internationals in Leeuwarden** struggle more than residents in general (`01-desk-research-leeuwarden.md` §4), and whether the cause is **not knowing** the rules or **hassle**.

## 2. Recruitment message (copy and paste)

> Hi! I'm a CMD student at NHL Stenden working on a small project about waste sorting in Leeuwarden. 🗑️
> I'm looking for **internationals living in Leeuwarden** for a **15-minute video call** this week. You'll sort some everyday items and tell me about your experience. No preparation needed, and there are no wrong answers. It's the rules that are confusing, not you!

Aim for **6–8 people**. Note each person's country, how long they've lived in NL, housing type (student housing / shared / own place), and bin type (home bin / underground container).

## 3. Session script (15 min, on a video call)

**Consent (read out):** "I'll take notes and, if you're OK with it, record the screen. Your name won't appear anywhere. You can stop at any time."

### Part A: card sort, the pre-test (5 min)
Share a Miro or FigJam board with item cards and the bin columns, and ask them to drag each item where they think it goes. **Don't help, and don't correct them.** Then ask: "Which ones were you unsure about?"

**Draft items** (from the confusing items named in the municipal survey, p. 13, plus everyday student items). **Don't write in the correct bins until they've been checked on Omrin's own pages.**

Columns: the real Leeuwarden bins (check names and colours on Omrin) + **"Not a bin, something else"** + **"No idea"**.

### Part B: interview (8 min)
1. "Where are you from, and how long have you lived in Leeuwarden?"
2. "How does waste work where you grew up? What surprised you here?"
3. "**The last time you weren't sure where something went**, what did you do?" *(How do they look it up now?)*
4. "Have you ever had to get rid of **something big**, like furniture or a mattress? What did you do? **How did that feel?**"
5. "Do you know what a *milieustraat* is? Do you have a *milieupas*?" *(Don't explain until the end.)*
5b. "Have you ever seen Omrin's waste guide, the *Afvalwijzer*?" *(It exists in English. Tell them at the end.)*
6. "When you don't sort something, what's usually the reason?" *Offer the options, don't lead:* not sure where it goes · too much hassle · no space · other.

### Part C: close (2 min)
"Can I send you a 2-minute game to try on Friday?" → recruits for test 2.

## 4. What counts as validated

| Question | Validated if… |
|---|---|
| Internationals have a **knowledge** gap | Average card-sort accuracy below 70%, **or** at least half name "not sure where it goes" as a reason (Q6). Compare: 7% for residents in general. |
| Bulky waste is a real problem for them | At least 3 of 6–8 people have had it happen or worry about it (Q4), **or** most don't know the milieustraat or *milieupas* (Q5). |
| A game is better than a lookup | Q3 answers show they guess, ask a flatmate, or give up, rather than looking it up. |

**If the gap turns out to be hassle, not knowledge:** that's a valid finding too. Record it and adjust the concept (e.g. towards Friction! or a reminder habit) rather than ignoring it.
