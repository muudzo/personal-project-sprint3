## 2. Rules that matter for planning

- **Total programme:** 240 EC → Bachelor of Science. **156 EC are compulsory** competencies and **84 EC are free choice**.
- Competencies are always earned **in blocks of 3 EC** (3, 6, 9…).
- **Learn is capped at 30 EC over the whole study.** It can never be part of the free-choice space, so my remaining 3 EC Learn N2 has to come from the compulsory allocation.
- **Main phase:** every competency is earned once at N2, plus extra N2 or N3 credits of my choice.
- **Graduation (N3) mandatory block:** Create 6 · Learn 3 · Research 6 · Choice 15 = 30 EC.
- **Main phase also contains a 6 EC "N3 Research / Choice" block.**
- **Graduation access:** 150 EC in the main phase, **including all mandatory N2 and N3 EC.** My open N2 credits block graduation until they're done.
- **Internship access:** propaedeutic year + **45 EC at N2** + 2–3 minors completed.
- **Cum laude:** **21 EC at N3 before graduation** (the 6 required + 15 extra), a passing graduation assessment, and a nomination.
- **External minors** cannot award N1 or N3 credits or mandatory competencies (they become a 30 EC block).

## 3. The five competencies (one-line definitions)

| Competency | In short |
|---|---|
| **Research** | Investigate the problem; test, improve, and implement the solution. |
| **Create** | Develop concepts and turn them into designs and multimedia solutions. |
| **Communicate** | Get the message across to clients, peers, stakeholders, and experts. |
| **Organize** | Manage the work process, individually and in multidisciplinary teams. |
| **Learn** | Reflect on own work and actions; share knowledge and skills. |

A competency = **Knowledge** (knowing) + **Skills** (doing) + **Attitude** (mindset). Learning activities should always combine all three.

**Levels:** N1 = Operational · **N2 = Tactical** · **N3 = Strategic**.

## 5. CMD Canvas template (one per competency)

The growth portfolio needs a filled-in **CMD Canvas per competency**, discussed with the mentor. Copy this block for each one.

```markdown
### CMD Canvas: <Competency> @ <N2/N3>: <EC> EC

**Reason why:** Why do I want to learn this?
**Learning outcome (general):** <paste the level description from §4>
**Learning goals (SMART):**
- …
**Feedback so far:** Summary of previously received feedback / feedforward

#### Learning activities (the "how")
- **Inspiration:** Which examples will I study? Where and how do I get inspired?
- **Theory:** Which books, articles, documentaries, etc. will I study?
- **Creation:** What will I make? (products, artefacts, prototypes, sketches)
- **Consultations & feedback:** Which experts / consultants will I talk to?

**Summary: learning outcome in context:** Rewrite the learning outcome SMART for this specific project and situation (who, what, where, how).

**Evidence for the assessment:** What will I show to prove each checklist row in §4?
```

**SMART** = Specific · Measurable · Acceptable · Realistic · Time-bound.

## 8. Rules for any AI helping with learning goals

1. **Always state competency, level, and EC** (in 3 EC blocks) for every goal.
2. **Check each goal against the §4 checklist for its level.** N2 and N3 differ in specific words: *complex* vs *undefined*, *regularly* vs *continuously*, *thoughtful* vs *inspiring*. Use the right level's language.
3. **Every goal needs evidence.** Name what will be shown at the assessment.
4. **Make goals SMART** and tie them to the actual project (see `PROJECT_BRIEF.md`).
5. **Priority:** my open **N2 credits (Organize 6, Create 6, Learn 3)** come first. They block graduation.
6. **Keep Learn under the 30 EC lifetime cap.**
7. Update Section 1 after every assessment.
